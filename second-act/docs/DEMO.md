Use a real permission-cleared case if available; otherwise retain **synthetic inventory** and **fixture evidence** labels on screen and in narration. Do not present fixture mode as live Qloo/Nemotron.

## Recording evidence

For the final event demo, prefer a successful live Qloo run and live Nemotron draft. Keep a redacted record of timestamp, provider/model, request purpose, response provenance, and the decision result. Never show keys, authorization headers, private contract documents, or identifiable audience data.

The fixture narration is suitable for an honest development preview. It is not a substitute for either event's required live provider integration. Do not submit until the relevant checklist is complete.
